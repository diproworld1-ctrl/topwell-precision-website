About Topwell Precision

One factory, seven steps, serving buyers worldwide since 2017

Our Story

Built for buyers who can't afford surprises

Founded in 2017 in Jiaxing, Zhejiang Province, Jiaxing Topwell Precision Machinery Co., LTD is a dedicated sheet metal fabrication manufacturer serving international buyers in Europe, North America and beyond.

We operate a full in-house production chain: laser cutting, tube laser cutting, CNC bending, welding, polishing, surface treatment and assembly. Every step happens under one roof — which means one quality standard, one lead time, one supplier to hold accountable.

Our location in the Yangtze River Delta — 30 minutes from major ports and rail hubs — gives us fast, cost-effective logistics to EU and US destinations.

We don't promise "best quality, best price." We promise: exact tolerances, on-time delivery, and honest communication.
